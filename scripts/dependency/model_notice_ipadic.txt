===========================================================================
Training data: UD Japanese-GSD
===========================================================================

The model was trained on the train file of the Universal Dependencies
treebank UD Japanese-GSD

  https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Japanese-GSD

which is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International license

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The sentences of the treebank were segmented again with IPADIC, its
bunsetsu and their dependencies were projected onto those morphemes, and
the weights of the model were estimated from the result. The model holds
the names of features and their weights. A name is made of a part of
speech, a conjugation form or the surface of one morpheme, so single words
that occur in the sentences are in the file. No sentence is, and no
annotation of the treebank: no bunsetsu and no dependency.

The Lindera project takes the position that such a model is not an
adaptation of the treebank, and distributes it under the license above.
No court has decided this question. What the position rests on, and what
is uncertain, is in the documentation of lindera-dependency:

  https://lindera.github.io/lindera/lindera-dependency/model_file.html
